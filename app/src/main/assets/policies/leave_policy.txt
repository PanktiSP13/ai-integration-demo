Leave Policy

Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Medical Leave:
Employees are entitled to 12 days of medical leave per calendar year.
Medical leave may require supporting medical documentation as defined by company procedures.

Casual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 6 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Leave Carry Forward:
Unused annual leaveg may be carried forward according to company policy.
